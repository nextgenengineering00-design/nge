NGE v27 - Language switch

Added a compact TH / EN / 中文 language selector to the public website header and mobile menu.
- Thai remains the original/canonical website language for SEO.
- English and Simplified Chinese translate the visible page automatically.
- Selected language is remembered across page changes.
- Works on legacy pages, local service/area pages and project detail pages.
- Brand name, phone and LINE labels are protected from automatic translation where possible.

Important: this is an on-page translation layer, not separate indexable /en or /zh SEO pages.
If multilingual SEO is needed later, create dedicated English and Chinese URLs with translated metadata and hreflang.
